# Casedoku Manor — Marketing Capture Language

Corrected marketing + social-video copy that keeps the strongest hook while avoiding the structural fiction problem (suspects recur as **markers**, like numbers in Sudoku — never imply literal people standing in many rooms). The grid is **Inspector Graves' deduction METHOD**; the **accusation** is the concrete payoff; solving **restores the manor**.

---

## Approved vocabulary (USE / AVOID)

**USE — the hook + the explainers:**
- **"Sudoku, but every number is a suspect."** ← the strongest hook; lead with it.
- "Place suspect markers."
- "Complete deduction grids." / "Complete Graves' deduction grid."
- "Follow evidence." / "Follow evidence before you accuse."
- "Close cold cases."
- "Restore the manor." / "Every solved case restores the manor."
- "You don't just finish the grid. You close the case."
- "The grid is complete. Now close the case."

**AVOID — anything implying literal repeated people / locations:**
- ✗ "Find where every suspect was"
- ✗ "Track where everyone stood"
- ✗ "Place suspects in the rooms where they were"
- ✗ "Recreate the crime scene"
- ✗ (general) "where they stood," "the room they were in," "the floor plan," "a map of the manor"

**Rule of thumb:** the marker *recurs* (a deduction), it does not *stand* somewhere (a place). Talk about completing the **grid** and following the **evidence**; let the **accusation** be where it gets concrete (culprit, weapon, motive, proof).

## Production notes (for capturing the clips)

- **Clean frames:** press **Ctrl+Shift+C** in-game for the chrome-free **capture view** (hides toolbar/buttons/HUD, keeps the board, tray, clue, the "🔍 Inspector Graves' Deduction Grid" caption, and the clock).
- **Look:** Settings → **Atmosphere: Focus** for a calmer board; **Motion ON** for the juicy reveals (payoff stamps, the suspect-focus lens, restoration), **Motion OFF** for crisp stills.
- **Staging the beats:** with the dev panel (`?dev=1`) you can drive the moments on demand — **Replay opening intro** (folder slide-in), **Fill solution** (a completed grid), **Jump to accusation**, **Win current instantly** (the case-closed payoff), plus the manor restore controls.
- **Real moments to shoot:** the case-file folder slide-in · placing markers on the deduction grid · the **suspect-focus lens** (tap a suspect → its markers light, the board dims to one thread) · the **clue-to-board section highlight** (a clue lights its room section + tray marker) · the **accusation** (name killer / prove weapon / prove motive / the clue that breaks the alibi) · the **case-closed payoff** cascade · the **manor restoration** before/after.

- **manor restoration** → the Case Seal → "Restore the [Room]" → before/after room reveal → Graves clue.
