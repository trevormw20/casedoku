# Cludoku — Weapon Generation Prompts

**How to use:** Attach `assets/reference/style-reference.png` to every generation so weapons match the locked Cozy Cartoon Noir style. Generate on plain flat white, then cut out to transparent PNG. Save each as `weapon-<name>.png`, 512×512.

**Display rules (so they read as one set):**
- 1:1 square canvas, weapon centered with generous empty margin (shapes vary a lot — the padding is what keeps them uniform in a row).
- Transparent cutout, NO baked background or scene. The app supplies the backdrop: a cream/aged-paper "evidence card" with a soft shadow and a label, or a warm spotlight for the reveal.
- Same slight angle, one soft drop shadow, and a similar visual scale on every weapon (so the knife isn't dwarfed by the candlestick).

Each prompt below is complete and copy-paste ready.
