    Follow the clues. Complete the grid. Then close the case.

    It's a sudoku you solve like a detective. The starting clues are not enough on their own — that's on purpose. You need both sudoku logic and the evidence. Use them together.

    1. Place the suspect markers on Graves' grid so each one shows up exactly once in every row, every column, and every room (a 3×3 block).
    2. Use the evidence to work out what sudoku alone can't. The clues break the ties the grid leaves open. (You can't win by sudoku alone.)
    3. Accuse once the rest of the grid is filled in. Name the killer, the proof, and the motive. The dashed scene is the victim's spot — only your accusation fills it.

    Tap a cell to pick it, then tap a suspect marker to place it. ★ marks key evidence — you need it to solve the case. The rest just back it up. Every clue is true.
