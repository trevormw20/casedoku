    How to Play

    A sudoku you finish with detective work. The starting clues are intentionally not enough on their own — sudoku logic and the evidence work together, interleaved.

    1. Place the suspects so each appears exactly once in every row, every column, and every room (3×3 block).
    2. Use the evidence to resolve what sudoku alone can't — the clues break the ties the grid leaves open. (You can't finish by sudoku alone.)
    3. Make your accusation once the grid is complete: name the culprit, the proof, and the motive.

    Tap a cell to select it, then tap a suspect to place them. ★ marks key evidence (required); the rest corroborate. Every clue is true.

    Final Deduction

    The house is in order and every guest is placed. Detective — make your accusation.

    1 · Who is the culprit?
    2 · What proves it?
    3 · What was the motive?
