# Casedoku — Branding & Expansion Map

**Master brand: CASEDOKU** (verified clear; theme-neutral; friendly tone across all packs).

A murder-mystery deduction-sudoku game. The brand is built on the mechanic, not the setting, so every themed expansion lives under one name. The wordmark and logo stay constant; each pack swaps the art and carries its theme in a subtitle.

## Naming structure
**Casedoku: <Theme>**

- The base game launches as **Casedoku: Manor** (the cozy-noir mansion theme — current art direction).
- Each DLC/season is a new subtitle. The "CASEDOKU" wordmark never changes; only the subtitle and the art/story layer do.
- Tone rule: the master brand avoids "murder/kill" so it stays friendly for lighter packs (kids, animals). Packs can flavor their own copy as needed.

## What stays constant vs. what reskins
- **Constant:** the CASEDOKU wordmark, the core sudoku-deduction engine, the 3-layer board system, UI layout, difficulty tiers.
- **Reskins per pack:** suspect cast, room floors, objects, weapons/items, story library (names, motives, victim, "weapon," locations), color accents, and the subtitle.

This is why the art is built data-driven and slot-based (see ART_BIBLE.md): a new pack is the same asset slots, redrawn.
