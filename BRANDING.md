# Casedoku — Branding & Expansion Map

**Master brand: CASEDOKU** (verified clear; theme-neutral; friendly tone across all Case Archives).

A cozy cold-case deduction-sudoku game — solve the mystery, restore the manor, uncover the truth. The brand is built on the mechanic, not the setting, so every themed expansion lives under one name. The wordmark and logo stay constant; each Case Archive swaps the art and carries its theme in a subtitle.

## Naming structure
**Casedoku: <Theme>**

- The base game launches as **Casedoku: Manor** (the cozy cold-case mansion theme — current art direction).
- Each DLC/season is a **Case Archive** — a new subtitle. The "CASEDOKU" wordmark never changes; only the subtitle and the art/story layer do.
- Tone rule: the master brand avoids "murder/kill" so it stays friendly and cozy across all Case Archives (including lighter ones — kids, animals). Each Archive can flavor its own copy as needed.

## What stays constant vs. what reskins
- **Constant:** the CASEDOKU wordmark, the core sudoku-deduction engine, the 3-layer board system, UI layout, difficulty tiers, and the Relaxed / Detective play modes.
- **Reskins per Case Archive:** suspect cast, room floors, objects, weapons/items, story library (names, motives, victim, "weapon," locations), color accents, and the subtitle.

This is why the art is built data-driven and slot-based (see ART_BIBLE.md): a new Case Archive is the same asset slots, redrawn.
