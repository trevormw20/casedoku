# Casedoku — UI & Branding Generation Prompts

**How to use:** Match the locked Cozy Cartoon Noir style (attach `assets/reference/style-reference.png` for line/palette/shading where it helps). Palette: noir base (ink navy #1B2330 / charcoal #2A2F3A), warm woods, cream case-file paper (#F2E8D5 / #E8D9B5), gold accent (#E0B04A), warm-black outline (#1A1A22). Icons should be traced to SVG after generating; everything else exports as the size noted. Transparent PNG unless stated.

---

## Logo / Wordmark — logo.png (~2048px wide, transparent)
Generate a game logo wordmark reading "CASEDOKU" in cozy cartoon noir style: bold rounded hand-drawn cartoon lettering with a thick warm-black outline (#1A1A22), filled in warm gold (#E0B04A) and cream (#F2E8D5) with a subtle two-tone shadow, a playful but legible detective vibe, subtle paper grain. Centered, transparent background, no extra scenery, no tagline. Wide horizontal lockup.

## Subtitle lockup — logo-subtitle.png (optional)
Generate the word "MANOR" as a small companion subtitle in the same cozy cartoon noir lettering, thinner weight, cream on transparent, sized to sit under the CASEDOKU wordmark. (Swap the word per theme pack: Cosmos, Camelot, Noir, etc.) Transparent background, no other elements.

## Main background (desktop) — bg-desktop.jpg (1920×1080)
Generate a cozy detective's desk scene from a slight top-down angle, cozy cartoon noir style: a warm pool of light from a desk lamp, scattered case-file folders, a magnifying glass, a corkboard with pinned notes and red string in the soft-focus background, muted ink-navy and warm-wood palette, bold outlines, flat shading, subtle paper grain. Atmospheric but uncluttered in the center so UI can sit on top. No text, no people.

## Main background (mobile) — bg-mobile.jpg (1080×1920)
Same cozy detective's desk scene in cozy cartoon noir, recomposed vertically: desk lamp glow and case files toward the bottom, corkboard with pinned notes and red string up top, clear muted space in the middle for UI. Muted ink-navy and warm-wood palette, bold outlines, flat shading, subtle paper grain. No text, no people.

## Difficulty badges — badge-easy / badge-medium / badge-hard / badge-extreme.png (512×512, transparent)
Generate a set of four enamel-pin style rank badges in cozy cartoon noir, matching each other: a detective's badge/shield emblem with bold black outline, flat two-tone shading, subtle paper grain, transparent background, no text. Tier color coding — Easy: friendly green; Medium: gold; Hard: orange-red; Extreme: deep plum/charcoal with a small star. Keep all four the same shape and size, only the color/accent changes.

## "Case Closed" stamp — stamp-caseclosed.png (512×512, transparent)
Generate a cartoon rubber-stamp graphic reading "CASE CLOSED" in a circular/oval frame, cozy cartoon noir style, distressed red ink look, bold outline, subtle paper grain, slightly rotated, transparent background. (For the killer-reveal screen.)

## Killer spotlight frame — frame-spotlight.png (512×512, transparent)
Generate a warm circular spotlight/vignette frame in cozy cartoon noir to sit behind the revealed culprit: a soft golden glow ring with a darker vignette around it, bold simple edge, transparent center and background so a character portrait shows through. No text.

## Evidence card frame — ui-evidence-card.png (512×512, transparent)
Generate a single empty "evidence card" tile in cozy cartoon noir: a cream/aged-paper rounded rectangle with a soft drop shadow, a small paper-clip or evidence-tag detail in one corner, bold outline, subtle paper grain. Empty center (a weapon/object cutout will be placed on top). Transparent background outside the card.

## UI icons — icon-<name>.png (512×512, transparent, then trace to SVG)
Generate a matching set of simple UI icons in cozy cartoon noir: a single warm-black (#1A1A22) rounded line on a transparent background, consistent stroke weight, simple friendly forms, no fill color, no text. Generate each centered with margin. Set: undo (back-arrow), redo (forward-arrow), erase (eraser), hint (lightbulb or magnifying glass), check (checkmark), settings (gear), back (left chevron), close (X), notebook (case notebook), lock (padlock). Keep all icons the same visual weight and size so they sit together cleanly.

## App icon / favicon — appicon.png (512×512) + appicon-192.png (192×192)
Generate an app icon in cozy cartoon noir: a bold simple emblem combining a magnifying glass over a small 3×3 grid (the sudoku-detective mashup), warm gold and cream on an ink-navy rounded-square background, thick outline, flat shading, very legible at small sizes, no text. (favicon can be derived from this.)
